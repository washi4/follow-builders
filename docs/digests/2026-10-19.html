How we contain Claude across products
As agents gain more access, capping the blast radius through environmental containment is more reliable than model layer supervision.
• Anthropic uses three isolation patterns: ephemeral containers for claude.ai, human in the loop sandboxes for Claude Code, and sealed local VMs for Claude Cowork.
• Human supervision is fallible due to approval fatigue, making deterministic boundaries like egress controls and filesystem limits essential.
• Custom built proxies are often the weakest link, while battle tested hypervisors and syscall filters remain highly secure.
https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/how-we-contain-claude

An update on recent Claude Code quality reports

Anthropic resolved three separate issues that caused Claude Code to feel less intelligent and more forgetful over the past month.
• A change that defaulted reasoning effort to medium was reverted to high after users reported lower intelligence.
• A caching bug inadvertently cleared older thinking blocks on every turn, causing repetitive and forgetful behavior in long sessions.
• A system prompt update meant to reduce verbosity accidentally hurt coding quality and was quickly rolled back.
