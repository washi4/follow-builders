Training Data: Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed: Why AI Models Stop Learning, and How to Start It Again
Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed argue that true AI requires continual learning from experience, not just scaling static models.
- Current LLMs stop learning after post training, relying on frozen weights and synthetic data, which Sutton views as a fundamental bottleneck compared to learning from real world experience.
- The big world hypothesis suggests the world is infinitely complex, meaning agents must continually update their own models and abstractions rather than relying on human curated datasets.
- Oak Lab is focusing on continual deep learning and step size optimization to prevent catastrophic forgetting, aiming to build systems that learn and plan autonomously over time.
"The field is weird. They feel they need to call it continual learning. It's just learning."
